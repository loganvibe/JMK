# Student User Guide

> **Academic integrity:** jmk is a reference and learning tool. Everything the AI produces must be reviewed, verified and rewritten in your own words before submission. Always follow your supervisor's and institution's rules.

## 1. Create your account
Sign up with your email or Google. Check your inbox for the verification link, then sign in.

## 2. Complete your profile
Go to **Profile** and add your university, faculty, department, course, academic level and expected graduation year. This is what lets the AI match your department's methodologies and your school's formatting expectations.

## 3. Create your first project
From the dashboard, choose **Create project**. Tell jmk your area of interest and it will propose researchable topics with scope and difficulty. Pick one and your project workspace is created.

## 4. Work through your chapters
Inside the workspace you get a tab per section — Abstract, Chapters 1–5 and Defense Prep. For each section you can:
- **Generate** a first draft
- **Improve**, **Expand** or **Simplify** existing text
- Edit freely — everything autosaves to your project

Your progress bar updates as sections are completed.

## 5. Use the Academic Assistant
- **Research** — ask questions with your project as context
- **Citations** — build APA 7, MLA, Harvard or IEEE references and convert between styles
- **Quality check** — an 8-dimension academic score with fixes
- **Supervisor feedback** — paste your supervisor's comments to get them explained and actioned

## 6. Refine an existing project
Already have a draft? Use **Refine project** to upload a PDF or DOCX. jmk analyses structure, research quality and writing, asks a few questions, then rewrites section by section with a before/after comparison.

## 7. Prepare for your defense
The Defense tab gives you a readiness score, a 5- or 10-minute speech summary, a 10-slide presentation, a mock defense with an AI examiner, a defense coach and a final submission checklist.

## 8. Plans and credits
Every AI action uses credits. Your remaining credits are shown on the dashboard and on **Billing**. Free Trial covers Chapter 1 of one project; Student and Premium+ unlock all chapters, refinement, exports and (on Premium+) the mock defense simulator.

## Need human help?
**Services** lets you request paid, human-delivered work such as data analysis or app development. Submit your brief and an admin sends you a quote.
